Our Story

Built Different,
On Purpose.

Too many businesses pay for digital projects that look great in screenshots but do nothing in the real world — slow websites, vague deliverables, and agencies that vanish after launch. neunrg was built to be the opposite: an agency that thinks in outcomes, writes clean code, and stays accountable for results.

From our base in Rajkot, we serve manufacturing firms, retailers, service providers and startups across Gujarat, India and beyond. We're big enough to deliver serious engineering, and small enough that you talk directly to the people building your product.

Our name says it all: new energy. We bring that energy to every project — in how we design, how we build, and how we think about your business.
