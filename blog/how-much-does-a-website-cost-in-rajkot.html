How Much Does a Website
Cost in Rajkot?

neunrg Team • August 15, 2026 • 6 min read

"How much does a website cost in Rajkot?" is the first question almost every business owner asks — and it's the one almost nobody answers honestly. Some quote ₹5,000. Some quote ₹5,00,000. Both can be "right" for very different projects.

The truth is that website pricing depends on what you're actually buying. A simple information page, a store that processes payments, and a custom web application are three completely different products. This guide gives you real, honest ranges for Rajkot in 2026 — and shows you exactly what drives the cost up or down.

What determines the price of a website?

Before numbers, understand the five factors that create the price. Any quote you receive is built from these:

  • Type of project — brochure site, e-commerce store, portal, or web application. This is the biggest factor.
  • Design work — a custom design built around your brand costs far more than a template with your logo dropped in.
  • Features & integrations — payment gateways, WhatsApp integration, booking systems, admin dashboards, multi-language support.
  • Content & copy — who writes your text, shoots your photos and structures your pages.
  • SEO, speed & ongoing support — a website that isn't built to be found or maintained is a liability, not an asset.

Typical website costs in Rajkot (2026)

These ranges reflect what serious web development companies in Rajkot actually charge — not the ₹3,000 "websites" sold by WhatsApp spam.

Basic business website — ₹15,000 to ₹40,000

A 5–8 page site for a small business: home, about, services, contact, plus a form. Often template-based with light customization. Fine for credibility, but expect limited design and little SEO work.

Custom business website — ₹40,000 to ₹1,00,000

A unique design, 8–15 pages, copywriting, mobile-first development, on-page SEO and speed optimization. This is the range where most businesses in Rajkot should be looking — it's where you get a website that actually generates leads.

E-commerce store — ₹60,000 to ₹2,50,000+

Product catalogs, cart and checkout, payment gateway integration (Razorpay etc.), order and inventory management. The range depends on catalog size and how much of the backend is custom.

Custom web application or portal — ₹1,00,000 to ₹5,00,000+

Dashboards, client portals, booking systems, internal tools with logins, databases and integrations. These are software projects, and pricing follows complexity.

Why "cheap" websites end up expensive

Every web development company in Rajkot has clients who came to them after a ₹5,000 website failed. Here's how that usually plays out:

  • No mobile experience. Half your customers are on phones. A site that breaks on mobile quietly destroys your leads.
  • Invisible on Google. A website that nobody finds isn't cheaper — it's wasted money. Fixing SEO later costs more than building it in.
  • No ownership clarity. You may not own the code, the domain is in someone else's name, and you can't leave.
  • The agency disappears. Launch day is the last day you ever hear from them.

The real cost of a website is (price + the cost of downtime, rework and lost customers). That's why outcomes matter more than the quote.

Red flags when comparing quotes

  • A price with no written scope or itemized list.
  • Portfolio you can't verify — no live sites, no case studies.
  • Promises of "first page of Google" guaranteed.
  • Requests for 100% payment before any work begins.
  • No mention of support or maintenance after launch.
