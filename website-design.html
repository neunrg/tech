FAQ

Website Design Questions,
Answered.

How long does website design take?
A complete business website design typically takes 1–2 weeks including wireframes, mockups and two revision rounds. Larger products take longer.
Do you design mobile versions too?
Yes — we design mobile-first. Your website is designed, tested and refined on mobile screens before desktop.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Absolutely. We audit what's working, keep your brand identity where it's strong, and rebuild the experience for modern standards.
Do I own the design files?
Yes. On project completion you get full ownership of the design files, source code and all assets.
